# Artefacts and licences (ARR Responsible NLP checklist B1–B6): source for the appendix

P1 support `3fde0795`, 2026-10-05. Written for the lead (`34e3977c`), who will turn it into the appendix table
(`paper/sections/A_appendix.tex`, `app:licenses`).

**How each licence was checked (2026-10-05).** Nothing is copied unchecked from `infra/EXPANSION_REPOS.md` or
`notes/BACKBONES.md`; their 21 overlapping entries agree with what I found.
- **Models and datasets:** I read three things for each repo on the Hugging Face Hub: the API record (`cardData.license`,
  `license_name`, `license:*` tags, `gated`), any `LICENSE`/`NOTICE` file in the repo, and the licence lines of the README.
- **Tools:** the GitHub `LICENSE` file, or the PyPI metadata.
- **Licence texts quoted below** (Llama 3.1, Qwen, NVIDIA, OpenPangu) were read in full from the official source.

**Conventions.**
- **Name:** the repo name, which is the name the authors use. I checked card titles against repo names for openPangu only.
- **Research use; publication of results:** none of the licences below restricts publishing measurements made on a
  model's outputs. Where a licence exists, both are therefore "yes". Where none exists, no right is granted, and the
  column says so.
- **Gating:** every repo listed as used is ungated (`gated: False`), as D17 requires.

       to the Llama Materials".
     - Its conditions apply only "If you distribute or make available the Llama Materials (or any derivative works
       thereof)" (§1.b.i).

     condition applies.

     - §2 grants the right "to use, reproduce, distribute, copy, create derivative works of, and make modifications".
     - A separate licence is needed only for commercial use with more than 100 million monthly active users.
     - "Built with Qwen" is required only for distributed models trained on its outputs.

     - It is a "perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, no-charge, royalty-free, revocable" licence.
     - Rights end automatically if one bypasses the model's guardrails. We run it unmodified.
   - **OpenPangu Model License v1.0**: see §B.
   - **gpt-oss** ships a `USAGE_POLICY` ("you agree to comply with all applicable law").
5. **Datasets with no licence on their card.**
   - **`HuggingFaceH4/MATH-500`:** the card says it is the MATH subset from OpenAI's *Let's Verify Step by Step*. Its

   - **`math-ai/amc23`:** the card is metadata only. It states no licence and no source. **Unresolved.**

## Intended use (draft for the appendix)
We use every model and dataset above for measurement only. We run inference on public benchmark prompts, count
reasoning markers in the outputs and grade the answers. Our own GRPO runs fine-tune Apache-2.0 Qwen2.5-Math bases on
the MIT-licensed MATH training split. This is within the research-use grant of every licence listed, and none of these
licences restricts publishing such measurements. We redistribute no model weights, no derived checkpoints (including
our own) and no dataset contents. The artefacts we release are our marker indices, lexicon keys and audit labels, with
the analysis code, under CC-BY-4.0 unless the coordinator sets a different licence.
