## D50 (2026-10-05 ~17:30, lead 34e3977c) PRE-REGISTERED METHOD "Calibrated Wait" (CW), written before any method result exists
Merged from notes/METHOD_DESIGN_P1.md (former lead), notes/METHOD_DESIGN_draft.md (lead), the coder's feasibility check and gpt-6-astra's red-team (notes/ASTRA_method_redteam.md; discussant only).
- **Motivation (no new claim).** Under RL-Zero the inherited marker becomes a more liberal alarm: in OLMo-3 RL-Zero, wait follows 25.4% of error-free injected-error prefixes (base 19.9%) and 73.7% of erroneous ones (base 47.1%). The method tests whether moving the criterion back, while keeping sensitivity, saves tokens without losing accuracy. "Alarms on labelled-correct prefixes" are NOT assumed useless; the branch test (S5) checks it.
- **Method.** A single-pass vLLM V1 logits processor. At each sentence start (previous token ends a sentence, deterministic rule frozen in code before any run) it computes the gate score s = mean token entropy of the just-completed sentence, exact, from the full next-token distribution. If s < τ ("no error signal"), it masks the first tokens of every sentence-initial revision-marker form (the paradigm F, per-tokenizer variants) at that position only; otherwise decoding is unchanged. One feature, frozen; mean token logprob is an ablation only.
- **Calibration (per model, DEV only, never test).** 300 MATH-train L3–5 problems, the SDT clean/injected prefixes built from the model's own traces. τ is the largest value with hit rate ≥ 0.9 × the vanilla hit rate on erroneous prefixes (sensitivity kept). Frozen before test.
- **Models.**
  - PRIMARY (confirmatory): Olmo-3-7B-RL-Zero-Math, Olmo-3-7B-Think, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B, Qwen3-8B (thinking).
  - SECONDARY (reported, not in the primary criterion): Phi-4-reasoning-plus, Qwen3.5-9B.
- **Benchmarks.** MATH-500 (×1), AIME24+25 (×4), AMC23 (×4), GSM8K first 500 (×1). Each model's own template and decode. AIME capped at 16k for the weakest thinkers (disclosed). Same seeds across conditions.
- **Conditions** (one processor; only the gate differs):
  1. vanilla;
  2. sham (processor on, never masks: implementation control);
  3. NoWait (mask every F form at every sentence start; also the published marker list);
  4. random suppression, rate-matched within position bins (deciles of response position), at CW's realised per-model rate;
  5. late-reflection suppression (mask every F form after a dev-chosen position);
  6. budget forcing (cap, then force the answer; 2 caps bracketing CW's mean tokens);
  7. CW.
  Optional: DEER-style confidence early exit on the dense models, if ready by Oct 7.
- **Metrics.** Accuracy (math-verify), ALL generated tokens per response, marker rate, wall-clock, plus the DEV operating point (HR, FA, d′, c) before and after gating. Statistics: a paired problem-cluster bootstrap stratified by benchmark (seeds of a problem stay in one cluster); Holm over S2–S3.
- **Success criteria** (macro over the 4 primary models × 4 benchmarks):
  - S1 (efficiency without loss): CW cuts tokens by ≥ 15% vs vanilla AND the 95% CI lower bound of the accuracy difference is ≥ −1.5 points.
  - S2 (beats blanket suppression): CW accuracy > NoWait accuracy, CI excludes 0.
  - S3 (the gate is informative): CW accuracy > position-binned rate-matched random suppression, CI excludes 0.
  - S4 (recalibration, not muting; held-out DEV half): FA falls ≥ 10 points while HR ≥ 0.9 × vanilla; c rises; d′ within its CI.
  - S5 (mechanism; natural events): on ~300 natural marker events from held-out DEV problems (2 models: OLMo RL-Zero, R1-Distill-7B), each branched allow vs suppress (×2), events the gate would suppress show a smaller allow-minus-suppress accuracy gain than events it keeps (difference CI excludes 0).
- **Reporting.** Every condition and every criterion goes in the main results table, pass or fail.
  - S1 fails but S4 holds: a recalibrated alarm that does not save tokens.
  - S3 or S5 fails: the gate is not informative; reported as such.
